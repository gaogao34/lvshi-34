# Response Format

Write in the user's language. Use clear headings and short, direct paragraphs. Avoid generic disclaimers at the beginning; state the legal-analysis boundary once in the conclusion or where uncertainty matters.

## Compact format

Use this for a straightforward, low-stakes question.

1. **Preliminary view**: State the jurisdiction and the short answer, using qualified language such as "may," "typically," or "depends on."
2. **Potential rules**: List each plausible legal basis. For each one, say what conduct it regulates, what needs to be established, and how the known facts fit or do not fit.
3. **Possible consequences**: Separate civil, administrative, criminal, contractual, and employment/business consequences as applicable. Describe the type and range of outcome without inventing precision.
4. **What would change the answer**: List the missing facts or documents that are material.
5. **Recommended next steps**: Give lawful, proportionate actions and any deadline warning.

## Expanded format

Use this for complex or high-stakes matters.

### Assumptions and facts

State the jurisdiction, material facts supplied by the user, facts still uncertain, and any assumptions used. Do not label an assertion as fact merely because one party said it.

### Issue-by-issue assessment

For each issue, include:

- **Issue and risk level**: low, medium, high, or unable to assess, followed by a short reason.
- **Potential legal basis**: authoritative source and article if reliable; otherwise a description requiring source verification.
- **Conditions**: the legal elements, thresholds, or procedural conditions that matter.
- **Application to these facts**: what supports, weakens, or leaves the issue unresolved.
- **Potential consequences**: the legally available outcomes, separated by liability type.

### Evidence and timing

List useful evidence to preserve, missing documents, notification/response/limitation deadlines to check, and any fact that needs immediate confirmation. Recommend preserving originals and relevant metadata.

### Recommended next steps

Prioritize the next two to five lawful actions. Recommend prompt consultation with a licensed PRC lawyer for a high-risk, contested, or urgent matter.

### Boundary

End with one concise statement that this is a preliminary analysis based on supplied facts and that current official sources and the full record need review before a definitive legal conclusion is made.
